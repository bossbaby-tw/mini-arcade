Your hero can change. Everyone has a little of every kind of magic. Answers stay in this session.

A game, not a label.

These four fictional hero styles reflect the choices made during play. They are not validated personality types and do not predict mental-health risk.

Let children skip anything, pick more than one answer out loud, or play with a trusted adult. The feelings stops are invitations to talk, not scored questions. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

Ask “Does this feel like you today?” and celebrate differences. Avoid comparing scores or using the result to make decisions about a child.
